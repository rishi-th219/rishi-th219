### Production Case Study (Flagship Enterprise Experience)

#### **Statewide Healthcare Attendance & Payroll Intelligence**
*Data Analyst / BI Developer Intern · **CHiPS (Govt. of Chhattisgarh)** · 2025–2026*

* **The Problem:** Reconciled statewide public health biometric logs (AEBAS) against State Treasury payroll codes to eliminate ghost shifts and salary disbursal leakage across public health institutions.
* **The Architecture:**
  - Automated extraction of multi-source databases into staging via complex SQL CTEs and string-matching algorithms.
  - Architected a centralized **Kimball Star Schema** (`Fact_Attendance_Daily` surrounded by normalized Date, Facility, and Employee dimensions).
  - Formulated advanced **DAX measures** and a **3-tier attendance risk model** (High Risk Defaulter $\ge$ 3 consecutive unexcused absences).
  - Designed interactive executive **Power BI cockpits** with multi-level drill-downs for the Health Department Secretary.
* **Impact:** Eliminated operational blind spots for state executive leadership and ensured synchronized reporting coverage across public health facilities.
